# Timeline

A chronological view of the site's central themes. Full details are in the linked chapters.

## Polysilicon market

```mermaid
timeline
    title Demand and market (polysilicon)
    1995 : ~90% semiconductors
         : ~10% photovoltaics
    2014 : Solar share dominates
         : Demand ~18× vs 1995
    2004 : 11 plants worldwide
    2010 : 61 plants — oversupply
```

More context and Bernreuter charts in [Polysilicon](/en/polissilicio).

## Transistor architecture

```mermaid
timeline
    title Silicon transistors
    1960 : Planar MOSFET
    2003 : Strained silicon (Intel 90 nm)
    2007 : High-K metal gate (45 nm)
    2011 : FinFET / Tri-Gate (22 nm)
    2022 : GAAFET / nanosheets (3 nm)
    2025 : BSPDN (PowerVia, Super Power Rail)
    Future : Stacked CFET (&lt;1 nm)
```
